❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

硬件钱包真的有必要吗？Do I really need a hardware wallet?

如果你持有的金额值得保护、或打算长期持有，就有必要。热钱包私钥联网，面临钓鱼与恶意软件风险；硬件钱包私钥永不触网，签名在设备内完成。If your holdings are worth protecting or you're holding long-term, yes. Hot-wallet keys live online, exposed to phishing and malware; hardware-wallet keys never touch the network — signing happens inside the device.

助记词丢了会怎样？What if I lose my seed phrase?

助记词是资产的最终凭证：丢了等于丢币，泄露了等于送人。离线抄写在防火防水介质上，绝不拍照、绝不存云端、绝不告诉任何人。The seed phrase is the ultimate credential: lose it and the coins are gone; leak it and they're someone else's. Write it offline on fire/water-resistant material — never photograph it, never store it in the cloud, never tell anyone.

带蓝牙的硬件钱包安全吗？Are Bluetooth hardware wallets safe?

蓝牙只传输待签名的交易数据，私钥不出设备，签名在安全芯片内完成。主要风险在配对环节：只在可信环境配对，手机不 root/越狱更稳妥。Bluetooth only carries unsigned transaction data — keys never leave the device; signing happens in the secure chip. The main risk is pairing: pair only in trusted environments, and avoid rooted/jailbroken phones.

二手硬件钱包能买吗？Can I buy a second-hand hardware wallet?

绝不建议。二手设备可能被预置助记词或植入恶意固件。只从官网或官方授权渠道购买，收到后检查防拆封条并重置设备。Never recommended. Used devices may come with pre-set seed phrases or tampered firmware. Buy only from official sites or authorized resellers; check tamper seals and reset the device on arrival.

无种子（seedless）钱包靠谱吗？Are seedless wallets trustworthy?

Tangem、Cypherock 等用分片或多卡备份替代传统助记词，降低了抄写丢失风险，但恢复逻辑依赖厂商方案。适合怕丢助记词的新手；大额资产仍建议传统助记词 + 钢板备份。Tangem, Cypherock and peers replace seed phrases with sharded or multi-card backup — lower risk of losing paper, but recovery depends on the vendor's scheme. Good for beginners afraid of losing seeds; large holdings still deserve a classic seed + steel backup.
