❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

热钱包和冷钱包应该怎么分工？How should hot and cold wallets divide the work?

热钱包（软件钱包）负责日常交互：转账、DeFi、空投、NFT，只放小额和常用资金；冷钱包（硬件钱包）负责长期囤币，大额资产永远离线。被盗案例里九成以上丢的是热钱包里的钱 —— 具体选哪款冷钱包，看我们的硬件钱包横评。Hot wallets (software) handle daily activity — transfers, DeFi, airdrops, NFTs — holding only small, working amounts; cold wallets (hardware) hold long-term savings, with large assets always offline. Over 90% of theft cases lose hot-wallet funds — for picking a cold wallet, see our hardware wallet showdown.

软件钱包内置 swap 的费率到底收在哪里？Where does the built-in swap fee actually go?

Trust Wallet 圣诞事故之后，现在安全吗？Is Trust Wallet safe now after the Christmas incident?

The team rolled back to v2.69, mobile was never affected, and full reimbursement was promised (in progress as of 2026-10-07). A pipeline failure, not a cryptographic flaw. Extension users: confirm v2.69+ before use.
