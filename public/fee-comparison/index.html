❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

挂单 (Maker) 和吃单 (Taker) 有什么区别？What is the difference between maker and taker?

挂单是把限价单挂在订单簿上等待成交（提供流动性），吃单是直接吃掉订单簿上的现有单子（拿走流动性）。交易所通常给挂单更低的费率，鼓励做市。 Makers place limit orders that rest on the book (adding liquidity); takers fill existing orders (removing liquidity). Exchanges usually charge makers less to encourage market making.

Binance 的 BNB 抵扣值得开吗？Is Binance's BNB discount worth enabling?

用 BNB 抵扣现货手续费打 75 折（0.10% → 0.075%），合约吃单 9 折。如果你本来就持有 BNB，相当于白捡的折扣；专门为此买入 BNB 则要考虑币价波动风险。 Paying in BNB cuts spot fees by 25% (0.10% → 0.075%) and futures taker fees by 10%. Free discount if you already hold BNB; buying BNB just for this adds price risk.

Bitfinex 零费率是永久的吗？Is Bitfinex zero-fee permanent?

这是 Bitfinex 自 2025 年 12 月起的标准费率，不是限时活动。但交易所保留调整权利，大额交易前建议去官网再确认一次。 It has been the standard schedule since December 2025, not a limited promo. The exchange can still revise it — re-check the official site before large trades.
