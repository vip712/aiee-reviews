❓ 常见问题❓ FAQ

空投需要花钱吗？Do airdrops cost money?

大多数空投本身免费，但领取和交互需要支付链上 gas 费。任何要求你“先转账才能领取”的都是诈骗。Most airdrops are free, but claiming and interacting cost on-chain gas. Any "send funds first to claim" request is a scam.

没赶上快照还能参与吗？Can I still join if I missed the snapshot?

已经快照的空投无法补救。把精力放到新的积分 farming 项目上，尽早参与。Snapshotted airdrops can't be joined retroactively. Focus on new points-farming programs and start early.

为什么 Sonic 的未领取代币会被销毁？Why are Sonic's unclaimed tokens burned?

Sonic Labs 于 2026-04-02 公告：2026-10-15 之后，Season 1 与 Season 2 的未领取份额将由合约销毁，任何地址都可触发，没有宽限期。Sonic Labs announced on 2026-04-02: after 2026-10-15, unclaimed Season 1 & 2 allocations are burned by the contract — triggerable by any address, no grace period.

领取时钱包提示“高风险”怎么办？What if my wallet flags a claim as high-risk?

立刻停下来，核对你访问的是否为项目官方域名。不要在仿冒网站上签名任何交易。Stop immediately and verify you're on the project's official domain. Never sign transactions on lookalike sites.

空投收益需要交税吗？Are airdrops taxable?

视你所在司法辖区而定，部分地区将空投视为应税收入，请咨询税务专业人士。It depends on your jurisdiction — some treat airdrops as taxable income. Consult a tax professional.
